Under the hood.

How it works, for the curious: every step a save goes through, the AI it uses only when it must, the agents that fix it, and how it keeps the family's data at home.

What it is

Find every save. Name it. Look it up. File one note.

Finds

Wherever it was saved

Sent from a browser or a phone's Share menu, or read from Instagram, Chrome bookmark folders, exports and the inbox.

Knows

From public sources

What it is, from TMDb, OMDb, Wikidata, Common Sense Media and Open Library; and where to watch, read or hear it.

Files

In your own vault, on every phone

One note per title in the family's callimachus vault, filed straight away and synced to every phone; you correct it, and it re-files.

Films, short films, series, online video, articles, books, podcasts, and now visual art; a list post files every entry, and a trilogy files every film.

Filed the moment it arrives. (And a backup run at 6:30 a.m.)

01CaptureRead each source, newest first, to its checkpoint.
02LedgerSkip what was judged before. Never twice.
03ClassifyMedia? Which title? Code first, then the local model.
04ResolveOne canonical id: TMDb or Open Library.
05Where to watchA streaming service, the open web, or archive.org.
06EnrichFacts, registry links and the poster, from free sources.
07Check imagesA nudity filter at home. Library done; new saves soon.

A healthy run is plain code plus a free model on the home network: no paid AI, no agent, nothing to approve.

Filed at once, corrected by a human judge

Two questions on every card.

One

Is the record right?

The kind, the title, the catalogue entry. Approve it, pick candidate 1, 2 or 3, or say what it should be.

Two

Can you watch, read or hear it in one step?

“Consumable by human quickly and easily”: a link that plays, or the list of where it streams.

A judge reads every card first and passes on only the unsure ones. A best guess is filed at once and marked a guess; your pick re-files the same note, never a second one.

callimachusPart two · AI only where needed24 / 32

Healthy, degraded, stopped

Always one of three states, and a fuse.

Healthy

Every item finished by code or the free local model. The usual day.

Degraded

Correct notes, but something ran above plain code. An open signal drives a fix; 14 days at most.

Stopped

A capability is held. Capture keeps safely storing saves; the rest waits for a person.

The fuse blows on runaway numbers (more than 10 new fingerprints a day, 30 paid calls an hour, 5 agent sessions a day, any broken invariant or security trip) and stops every model call and agent launch until John resets it.

Repair and observe

Two loops, and John says yes.

maintainer: repair · on a signal

What broke, and how do we stop it?

A fresh session per signal: work around it today, reproduce it offline from the saved responses, fix it on a branch, replay every stored save, open one pull request and one card. Sonnet, then Opus. Never Fable.

maintainer: observe · daily, until settled

It works; how could it be better?

Recount the run, sample five notes against their sources, look for improvements under six headings, and propose each one as its own card. Haiku; Sonnet only if needed.

watchbill gives every agent a seat, a brief, a pinned model and a log. Every decision reaches John as its own card, with no cap.

callimachusPart two · Two loops29 / 32

What stays private

Your saves are yours. They're stored at home.

  • Saves are read from the family's own accounts by small, declared scripts in a sandbox.
  • Everything filed stays in the family's own vault, on their own Mac and phones, synced by a server on their own NAS.
  • Nothing is sold. Anthropic's Claude sees a save only while working on it: naming a title the local model could not, or an agent fixing a fault.
  • Karina's account is never read by a script: her saves come from her own export, or she sends them.
  • Sends arrive at one small door on the family's private network; it can only add a note to the inbox.
  • Public sources get a title or an id, never an account.
  • Paid AI sees a title question (the post's text, as data), never an account or session.
  • The code that holds a sign-in has no AI in it: injection there is impossible by construction.
  • GitHub holds the code and fixes, never the notes.
  • Posters are checked for nudity at home before any reaches a phone.
