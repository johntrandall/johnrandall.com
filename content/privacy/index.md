This page covers two things: the website johnrandall.com, and the LinkedIn developer application that John Randall operates to publish to his own LinkedIn profile.

## The website

johnrandall.com is a personal site. It serves static pages and PDF documents. A first-party analytics worker records page views and document downloads (path, time, referrer, coarse country, user agent) so John can see which links were read. No advertising, no tracking cookies, no third-party analytics, no sale or sharing of data.

## The LinkedIn application

The application is used by one person, John Randall, through his own tools, to post content to his own LinkedIn profile and to read his own LinkedIn identity. It is not offered to other members and does not collect, store or process any other member's data. The only LinkedIn data it holds is John's own access token and member identifier, stored in his password manager. It can be revoked at any time from LinkedIn's "Permitted services" settings.
